Privacy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Campus Tab has no accounts, no analytics and no server of its own. Everything it saves stays on your device. Nothing you do in it is collected, sold or shared.

What it saves, and where

All of this is stored only in your own browser (browser storage and IndexedDB on this device). It never leaves your device.

  • The school you picked and the accent colour
  • Your quick links, wallpaper choice and any wallpapers you upload
  • Campus photos fetched for your school
  • Clock, focus timer and widget settings

What it sends over the internet

  • The list of universities, loaded from the same site as the app when you open the school picker.
  • One lookup on Wikipedia when you pick a school, to find freely licensed photos of its campus (from Wikipedia, Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons). The request contains the school's name. It happens once per school; after that the photos load from your device. Wikimedia's own privacy policy covers those requests.
  • Links you click (your quick links, search, the apps menu) open those sites as any link would.

There are no cookies, ads, trackers or third-party analytics.

The Chrome extension's permissions

Installed as an extension, Campus Tab asks for these permissions. Each is used only to draw your new tab page, on your device.

PermissionUsed for
storage, unlimitedStorageSaving your settings and wallpapers on your device
bookmarksShowing your bookmarks bar on the new tab
topSitesShowing your most-visited sites
tabs, tabGroupsShowing your open tabs and tab groups, and switching between them
faviconShowing site icons next to links and tabs
alarms, notificationsLetting the focus timer tell you when a session ends

None of this information is read for any other purpose, stored anywhere else, or sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

Open Settings (the gear, top right) and choose Delete all my data at the bottom. That removes everything Campus Tab saved on your device. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing this site's data in your browser, does the same.

Children

Campus Tab is made for university students. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
